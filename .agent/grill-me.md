# /grill-me (Custom Command)

## Description

A relentless interview to sharpen a plan or design.

## Instructions

Interview me relentlessly about every aspect of this plan until we reach a shared understanding. Walk down each branch of the design tree resolving dependencies between decisions one by one. For each question, include your recommended answer. Ask one question at a time.

ref: https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/tree/main/skills/productivity/grill-me

# why i use this?

I use this when planing or design big task or project to close gap or things that i am missing to let ai keep interview me relentlessly
